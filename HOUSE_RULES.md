# House rules

These are the rules for everyone with an account on a Music Room server, and
for everyone who sends changes to its code. They are short on purpose.

## Your account

- One account each, in your own name. Don't sign in as anyone else.
- Your account code signs your laptop in. Keep it to yourself, like a key.
- Everyone starts as a listener. Artist, label, venue, business and shop
  accounts are switched on by the Music Room after a look.

## Music and pictures

- Only put up music you made, or have the rights to sell. That includes
  every sample in it.
- Only put up pictures, words and artwork that are yours, or that you have
  permission to use.
- A record you put up waits until the Music Room has looked at it. So does
  one you change after that.
- Your public room can play music only from the Music Room itself, or from
  the services' own players (Spotify, SoundCloud and others), which look after
  their own licences.
- If you see something that shouldn't be here, report it. The Music Room
  looks at every report and takes things down when they break these rules.

## Other people

- Be decent. Nothing hateful, threatening or cruel.
- Don't post anyone's private details.
- Don't try to get at other people's accounts, records, money or data, or at
  the server the records live on. Finding a weak spot is welcome; using one
  isn't. Tell the Music Room instead (see SECURITY.md).

## Changing the Music Room

- The code is open. You can read it, change your own copy, make plug-ins and
  themes, and shape your own room and public room however you like.
- Changes to the Music Room itself only go in after the Music Room has
  approved them. Every version that goes out is kept, so any of them can be
  brought back.
- Plug-ins see only what they say they need, and only after you switch them
  on.

## When things go wrong

- Use the feedback button to report a bug or say what you think.
- An account that breaks these rules can be paused.

The Music Room may change these rules. You will be told when it does.
