Music Room workshop
Make plug-ins, the look of your music room, and your own look of the app with
Claude Code, without changing the Music Room app itself.

The Music Room works like Ableton: the app is finished, and you add to it.
You make things in this folder; the app takes them in with its own buttons.
Your Music Room GR needs to be version 2026.11.27 or newer.

1. Put this folder anywhere you like (Documents is fine).
   Not inside a copy of the Music Room's code, if you have one.

2. Open Claude Code in this folder.
   In the Claude app: start a Code session and choose this folder.
   In a terminal: go into this folder and type  claude

   Claude reads CLAUDE.md in this folder by itself. It tells Claude how the
   Music Room takes plug-ins and looks, and the settings in .claude/ stop it
   touching the app or downloading its code.

3. Tell it what you'd like, in your own words. For example:
   "Make me a plug-in that shows every record for sale by where its seller is."
   "Make my music room feel like a late-night radio station."
   "Give the app a dark green theme."

4. To see it, in the Music Room:
   A plug-in:     Explore → Plug-ins → Add a plug-in… → choose its folder
                  (plugins/<its name>) → Switch on → Open.
                  After a change: Look again.
   Your room:     My Public Room → Code → Save as a file (keeps what you have),
                  then Open a file… → choose room/room.css.
   The app's look: Settings → Your own look → Open a file… → look/my-look.css
                  (⌘⇧0, or Ctrl+Shift+0 on Windows, opens Settings without it).
   A theme:       the ◐ key in the top bar → Import a theme… → themes/<name>.json

Claude won't change the Music Room app or make a new one. If it ever starts
to, stop it and say: "Read CLAUDE.md first."
