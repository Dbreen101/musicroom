MUSIC ROOM — a quick start
==========================

NOTE: Music Room comes in two builds — one for Macs with an Apple chip
(M1 or newer) and one for Intel Macs. Use the one you were sent; if it
won't open at all, you may have the other kind of Mac — just ask for the
other build. (Apple menu →  About This Mac shows which chip you have.)

1. Drag "Music Room.app" to your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).

2. FIRST LAUNCH: macOS will say it can't verify the developer and only
   offer "Done" or "Move to Bin". That's normal for an app shared outside
   the App Store. To open it:

      a. Double-click the app once (let macOS refuse it).
      b. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
      c. Scroll down — you'll see: "Music Room" was blocked…
         Click "Open Anyway", then confirm.

   You only need to do this once. After that, double-click as normal.

   If the button never appears, the Terminal route works too:
      Open Terminal, type  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine  followed by a
      space, drag the app into the window, press Return, then open the app.

3. Tell it where your music lives:
      → Tools → Settings → "Music library folder" → paste your music folder
        (an external drive, ~/Music, wherever). Music Room never moves or
        changes your files — it just reads them.

4. Import:
      → Tools → Import → Import. (Tick "Also analyse" for BPM/key/sound —
        slower but worth it. You can also do it later from Tools → Analyse.)

5. Wander into The Forest → The Well. Everything you imported is in there.

Stuck? There's a full, friendly guide inside the app: the "Help" tab.
And there's a feedback button in the corner of every page — anything odd,
confusing, or broken, send it straight from there.

Your data (library, artwork, notes) lives in:
   ~/Library/Application Support/Music Room
Back that folder up and you've backed up everything.
