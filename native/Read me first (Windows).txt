The Music Room GR — for Windows
============================

1. Unzip "Music Room GR (Windows).zip" somewhere you keep programs
   (for example your Documents folder). Keep the "Music Room GR" folder whole:
   the program needs everything beside it.

2. Open the folder and double-click "Music Room GR.exe".

3. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
   Press "More info", then "Run anyway". (The Music Room isn't signed by
   Microsoft yet; this happens once.)

4. Make your account at the front door. You need to be on the Green Room's
   Wi-Fi. If it can't find the server, press "change" under the form and type
   the address you were given.

The window is Microsoft Edge's (WebView2), which every Windows 11 has, and
Windows 10 has when Edge is up to date. Without it, the Music Room opens in
your browser instead, with a small window to quit it.

Your library and settings are kept in %APPDATA%\Music Room GR.
To make a shortcut: right-click "Music Room GR.exe" → Send to → Desktop.
