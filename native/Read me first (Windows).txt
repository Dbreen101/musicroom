The Music Room GR — for Windows
============================

The easy way: download "Install.Music.Room.GR.Windows.exe" from the release
page instead of this zip, open it, and press Install. Music Room GR opens by
itself, and stays in your Start menu and on your desktop.

With this zip:

1. Right-click the zip, choose "Extract All…", then "Extract". Opening
   "Music Room GR.exe" while it is still inside the zip does not work
   (Windows says "Failed to load Python DLL"): the program needs the whole
   "Music Room GR" folder beside it.

2. Open the extracted "Music Room GR" folder and double-click
   "Music Room GR.exe".

3. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
   Press "More info", then "Run anyway". (The Music Room isn't signed by
   Microsoft yet; this happens once.)

4. Make your account at the front door with the invite code you were given.
   Already have an account? Choose "I have an account" and type your account
   code instead. It works on any internet connection. If it can't find the
   server, press "change" under the form and type:
      https://musicroom.tail323912.ts.net

The window is Microsoft Edge's (WebView2), which every Windows 11 has, and
Windows 10 has when Edge is up to date. Without it, the Music Room opens in
your browser instead, with a small window to quit it.

Your library and settings are kept in %APPDATA%\Music Room GR.
