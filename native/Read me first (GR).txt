THE MUSIC ROOM GR — a quick start
=================================

There are two Mac downloads: "Apple Silicon" for Macs with an M1 chip or
newer (most Macs from late 2020 on), "Intel" for older Macs.
(Apple menu → About This Mac shows which chip you have.)

1. Unzip it and drag "Music Room GR.app" to your Applications folder.

2. FIRST LAUNCH: macOS will say it can't check the developer and only offer
   "Done" or "Move to Bin". That's normal for an app shared outside the App
   Store. To open it:

      a. Double-click the app once (let macOS refuse it).
      b. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
      c. Scroll down to: "Music Room GR" was blocked…
         Click "Open Anyway", then confirm.

   You only do this once. After that, double-click as normal.

   If the button never appears: open Terminal, type
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine
   then a space, drag the app into the window, press Return, and open it.

3. Make your account on the first screen (or sign in with your account
   code). The app already knows where the Music Room's server is — you need
   to be on the same Wi-Fi as it.

Help: the ? in the top bar. Something odd or broken: the feedback button
in the corner of every page.

Your data lives in:
   ~/Library/Application Support/Music Room GR
It is separate from any older "Music Room" app you may have, which is left
as it is.
