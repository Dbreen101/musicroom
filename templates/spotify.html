Music Room reads your Spotify library through their API and, once you sign in here, can play, pause and skip your own Spotify from the deck (Spotify allows that for Premium accounts). Spotify can see these requests. Tokens stay on this machine. No Spotify audio passes through Music Room: Spotify itself plays it. This page is also where you locate music you don't own, recognise what you already do, and trace it back to where the artist sells it.

Let the deck play your Spotify

You signed in before the deck could play, pause and skip Spotify for you. Sign in once more to allow it. Nothing needs changing on Spotify's developer page.

Connect a Spotify account

One-time setup. You'll need a free Spotify Developer app — it gives you a Client ID without a server-side secret.

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard.
  2. Create app → Web API. Set the redirect URI to Copy it exactly, number included. (Spotify's page calls an address without the number "not secure" and refuses it.)
  3. Copy the Client ID and paste it below.
