Music Room
1 · Your room

This is your room.

Your records, as a grid or a cloud, with your own tags, places and notes. Nothing here is recommended to you. You arrange it.

2 · Your files

Your music stays yours.

Bring in the files already on your laptop. The Music Room reads them, never moves them without asking, never uploads them anywhere.

3 · Borrow & buy

Borrow records. Keep them.

A borrowed record stays on your machine for good — it just only plays in here. Buy it any time and it becomes an ordinary file of yours, with what you've paid this month taken off the price.

4 · Other rooms

Walk into other rooms.

Artists, record shops, labels, venues — each with a room of their own. Borrow and buy straight from the people who made the music.
