Export

Move any part of your library out — to a folder on this laptop, a USB stick, or rekordbox / Engine for the booth. Hidden tracks (no title, or recorded sets) stay home unless included.

A place, pile or structure — to this laptop or a USB

Copies one place, pile or structure into Music Room/<its name>/ — a plain folder on this laptop, or onto a stick where it also rebuilds the stick's catalogue: a rekordbox XML pointing at the stick AND an Engine DJ database — plug straight into an SC Live / SC6000, or import the XML anywhere. Several places/piles can share one stick; each is a playlist. Re-exporting first shows what's new and what you've dropped since.

Into your export folder — audio + an .m3u8 playlist, no stick needed.

Everything onto a USB

Copies every playable, titled track into a flat Music Room folder on the stick (browsable on a Denon in folder mode), then writes musicroom_library.xml beside it pointing at those copies — import that one file in rekordbox or Engine on any laptop and the whole collection arrives with BPM, key and cue points. Re-running resumes: files already on the stick are skipped.

Export to rekordbox

Rewrites the rekordbox XML at its fixed local home (kept with the Music Room's own data, so it's always here even with the drive unplugged) — your whole collection, with every place and every structure as playlists, and your genres, tags and colours on each track (genres → Genre, tags/colours → Comments). BPM & key are left to rekordbox to analyse. It's then backed up to your drive alongside the database, the same way your music and metadata are. Point rekordbox → Preferences → Advanced → rekordbox xml at the local file once, then re-run to refresh.
