Anonymous telemetry — off by default

Off-device measurement, to design the app and see whether it drives real support to artists. It is opt-in and OFF by default, sends only aggregate monthly rollups of a small fixed list (never a per-event stream, never what you listen to or buy), behind an anonymous id that rotates every month, to a server you run.
